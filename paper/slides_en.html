Our probe: one mutation, 4× activity

R1107 (human) : ...UCGCAGCCC...   pos 30 = A   →  1×
R1108 (chimp) : ...UCGCGGCCC...   pos 30 = G   →  4× faster
69 nucleotides
1 mutation
4× activity factor

Skilandat et al. (RNA 2016) showed biochemically that the mutation breaks a P1/P1.1 base-pairing involving residues 9 and 60 (P1 helix endpoints).

Does the LM — which never saw any kinetics — place its predicted structural delta at the right place?

Honest verdict

✓ What we showed (defensible)

  • DRfold2 places the P1.1 ↔ P1 3D contact (~13 Å) correctly in its predicted structure.
  • It propagates the single-nt mutation through this contact in a mutation-specific way (0/20 random NULLs, pemp = 0.048).
  • Reproducible probing protocol (~30 min, ~€0.30).

✗ What this is not

  • Not a biological discovery. Mechanism already in Skilandat 2016.
  • Not proof that the LM "understands". Parsimonious explanation: correct geometry + local propagation.
  • No activity measurements done by us.

AlphaFold 3 cross-check — reported observation

All 7 sequences submitted to the AlphaFold Server (free, default settings). What AF3 itself reports:

AF3 pLDDT ≈ 25

Across the 7 sequences, AF3 reports mean confidence ~25 / 100. The ``low confidence'' threshold is 50. AF3 itself signals: ``I don't know.''

R1107 ↔ R1108 (1 nt diff)

DRfold2: ~1.86 Å global RMSD.
AF3: ~13 Å global RMSD, and Δ@9 = 39 Å. Two incompatible folds for 1 nt of difference.

Top-5 divergent residues

DRfold2: {9, 22, 24, 51, 60} (the P1).
AF3: {1, 8, 9, 10, 11} (5′ terminus). Only shared residue: 9.

Honest reading: AF3 on this short orphan-like ribozyme (~69 nt, no usable MSA) is not in a useful regime. The protocol surfaces this regime — itself a reportable observation. No biological interpretation drawn from AF3.

The NLP / transformer link

1. Micro-perturbation probing

Just as we ask BERT "did you learn syntax?" via a token swap, we ask DRfold2 "did you learn geometry?" via a nucleotide swap.

2. Self-supervised LM

DRfold2 is trained on raw RNA sequences, no functional labels. RCLM = transformer auto-encoder (BERT-style) on the AUGC alphabet.

3. Architecture matters

RhoFold+ (Evoformer, designed for MSA) in single-sequence mode: same top-5 regardless of mutation. Without MSA, it ignores the input → fixed prior.

4. NLP analogy

DRfold2 ≈ a BERT that correctly changes its prediction when you swap "the cat sat" → "the cats sat".
RhoFold without MSA ≈ a model that outputs the same sentence regardless of input.
